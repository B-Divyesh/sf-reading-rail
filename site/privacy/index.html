Privacy / effective 6 September 2026

Privacy for local reading support

Reading Rail works without an account and keeps its reading settings in browser storage.

What the extension stores

Reading Rail stores its on or off state, dim level, reading unit, and text spacing for each website hostname. The extension keeps these settings in local browser storage.

What it reads

The extension reads rendered text and its position to place the rail. It does not upload page text to Reading Rail.

Network and tracking

The extension makes no tracking or analytics requests. The website has no analytics or tracking scripts. Reading Rail does not use accounts, advertising identifiers, or third-party scripts.

Read aloud

When you select Read aloud, the browser receives the current text through its built-in speech feature. Voice availability depends on your browser and operating system.

Removing settings

Use “Reset this site” to remove settings for the current site. Uninstalling Reading Rail lets the browser remove all extension storage.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, this page will show a new effective date. Questions can be filed in the project’s public issue tracker.
