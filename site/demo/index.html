The public library team reviewed the website update after its Thursday test session.

Readers asked for clear labels before the catalogue search and account links.

The first change moves the “Renew books” action below the current loans list.

A second change adds a short explanation beside the address lookup field.

The team will test the revised page with three volunteers next Tuesday morning.

The notes link to the sample reference for the content checklist.

The checklist keeps dates, owners, and page sections in the same order as the review.

This makes the next handoff easier to scan after an interruption.

The final update records what changed and who will check it before publication.
