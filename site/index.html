Four useful parts / 02

A ruler, not a reader mode.

  1. 01

    Focus in place

    Choose one rendered line or the whole paragraph. The surrounding page dims while the original content remains live.

  2. 02

    Open the spacing

    Save Original, Open, or WCAG-width line and letter spacing per site. No font claims and no permanent page changes.

  3. 03

    Listen when useful

    Ask the browser’s built-in voice to read the current line. Stop it with the same button or shortcut.

  4. 04

    Stay on the keyboard

    Toggle, move, and read with shortcuts. Escape hides everything immediately, with no keyboard trap.

The privacy column / 03

Your reading stays between you and the page.

Reading Rail has no account, analytics, ads, network requests, or content upload. It stores only your settings for each site in your browser.
